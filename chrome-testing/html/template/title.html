<title>

Descriptive element — never rendered visually. Provides a short title for accessibility and tooltips. Browsers show the title as a tooltip on hover. The host shape always renders.

title on root svg (hover)
<svg> <title>Root SVG: Showcase...</title> <rect .../> </svg>
title child of circle
<circle cx="100" cy="60" r="50"> <title>Blue Circle...</title> </circle>
title child of g
<g> <title>Navigation icons</title> <rect.../> <rect.../> ... </g>
title with id + aria-labelledby
<svg aria-labelledby="chart-title"> <title id="chart-title"> Bar Chart: Monthly Revenue </title> </svg>
multiple title with lang
<polygon> <title lang="en">Warning...</title> <title lang="fr">Triangle...</title> <title lang="de">Warndreieck...</title> </polygon>
title never renders visually
<rect ...> <title> Never appears on canvas </title> </rect>

Note: <title> is a never-rendered element (UA sets display:none). Browsers expose its text as a tooltip on mouse hover and as an accessible name. The host shapes above all render correctly; only the title text is invisible on the canvas.
